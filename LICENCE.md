## 🧩 About

**Assassins Creed Shadows Companion** is a curated list of Assassins Creed Shadows open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, map markers, quest tracker
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — inventory catalog, gear manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — exploration layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Exploration Helpers
- Map Marker Overlay — highlight points of interest
- Quest Tracker — on-screen objectives and progress notes
- Fast Travel Notes — organize travel points
- Time of Day Control — adjust lighting for study (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎨 Inventory & Cosmetics
- Gear Catalog — browse weapons, armor, and cosmetics
- Custom Loadout Notes — organize player equipment
- Visibility Enhancer — improved contrast for exploration

### 🏋️ Combat Layout
- Practice Mode Notes — segment study of combat encounters
- Stance Switch Control — manually toggle stances mid-combat
- Dodge Buffer Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the editor
